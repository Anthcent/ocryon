# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers and library patrons using a phone or computer to digitize, search, and return to books and documents.

## Product Purpose

BiblioTru, for the Biblioteca Pública del Estado Trujillo, turns scanned pages into searchable text and keeps personal reading accessible. Success means reaching a saved page or starting a scan without friction.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, expressive, trustworthy. The experience should feel like an inviting public library while keeping tasks clear and direct.

## Anti-references

Generic login templates, 3D card stacks, dangling pull cords, persistent attention-seeking animation, and decorative effects that obscure the form.

## Design Principles

- Make the primary task unmistakable, especially on small screens.
- Let library identity invite exploration without competing with authentication.
- Explain capabilities with concrete actions, not abstract promises.
- Give interactions brief, meaningful feedback; avoid perpetual motion.
- Preserve a recognizable green identity across app surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, readable contrast, and a reduced-motion path. Mobile authentication should initially fit common phone viewports without scrolling while remaining usable on smaller screens and with zoomed text.
